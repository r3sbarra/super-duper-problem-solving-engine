## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-04

### Added
- **Unified Problem-Solving Architecture**: Integrated 8 classical inquiry paradigms:
  - John R. Platt's Strong Inference (exclusory experiment ranking).
  - C.S. Peirce's Triadic Discovery Cycle (Abduction -> Deduction -> Induction).
  - Kepner-Tregoe (KT) 4D Boundary Analysis with separating hyperplanes.
  - Altshuller's TRIZ (40 inventive principles & 39x39 contradiction matrix).
  - Goldratt's Theory of Constraints (TOC) & Evaporating Cloud resolution.
  - George Pólya's Heuristics (auxiliary problem decomposition & working backwards).
  - Gentner's Structure-Mapping Engine (SME) with Vector Symbolic Architecture (VSA).
  - Imre Lakatos's Epistemology of Mathematics (impossibility barrier auditing).
- **Continuous Latent Reasoning Substrate**:
  - Chain of Continuous Thought (Coconut) BFS latent reasoning.
  - Diffusion of Thoughts (DoT) non-autoregressive Classifier-Free Guidance denoising.
  - Negative Manifold Repulsor (NMR) geometric potential field for dead-end deflection.
  - Latent MCTS with Process Reward Model (PRM).
- **Formal Refutation & Proof Solvers**:
  - Pure-Python DPLL SAT refutation solver.
  - SMT Linear Real Arithmetic (LRA) Simplex solver and EUF congruence closure.
- **Autonomous Meta-Optimization**:
  - Causal SCM Pearl do-calculus intervention testing on internal parameters.
  - Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design (BOED) entropy-modulated parameter tuning.
  - Synaptic Memory Consolidation for dead-end cluster pruning.
- **Bridges & Extensions**:
  - `lab-ass` REST client and in-process hooks for agentic research workflows.
  - Scientific manuscript generator producing publication-ready Markdown and LaTeX.
- **Project Infrastructure**:
  - Apache-2.0 licensing, comprehensive test suite (53 tests passing), and GitHub Actions CI.
